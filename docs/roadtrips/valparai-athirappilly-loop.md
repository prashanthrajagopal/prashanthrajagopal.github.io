<h1 class="rt-title">Valparai – Athirappilly Forest Loop</h1>
<p class="rt-tagline">Coimbatore → Pollachi → Valparai (40 hairpins) → Malakkappara forest road → Athirappilly Falls → Thrissur → Palakkad → Coimbatore. ~400 km in a day, billed as South India&#x27;s most dangerous road.</p>

<p class="rt-summary">A one-day loop that climbs the famous 40-hairpin Valparai ghat, then takes the narrow Valparai–Malakkappara–Athirappilly road across the Anamalai and Sholayar forests — 85–90 km of single-lane tarmac with no fuel, no phone signal and a very real chance of elephants. It drops to Athirappilly Falls, Kerala&#x27;s biggest waterfall, and returns to Coimbatore across the plains via Thrissur and the Palakkad gap.</p>

<div class="rt-cols">
<div><div class="rt-panel "><h3>Highlights</h3><ul><li>The 40 hairpins from Aliyar dam to Valparai — each one numbered, with the Loop view point looking back over the dam.</li><li>Valparai tea estates and the Sholayar reservoir, one of the deepest dams in Asia.</li><li>The Malakkappara–Athirappilly forest road: 60-odd km of canopy, hornbills, gaur and the occasional elephant.</li><li>Athirappilly Falls — 80 ft wide, thundering in the months right after the monsoon.</li><li>Vadakkunnathan Temple in Thrissur and Palakkad Fort if you have daylight to spare.</li></ul></div><div class="rt-panel rt-offroad"><h3>Off-road &amp; motorcycling notes</h3><div class="rt-rating" aria-label="Off-road rating 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class=""></i><i class=""></i></div><p class="rt-lead">Tarmac all the way, but the forest stretch is single-lane, badly patched in places and completely unsupported. This is where an ADV with decent ground clearance and knobbly-ish tyres earns its keep.</p><ul><li>Valparai ghat hairpins are tight but well-surfaced; the danger is estate buses and Tempo Travellers taking the whole road.</li><li>Malakkappara–Athirappilly: potholes and landslide scars, moss on the shaded sections, and gravel at the culverts. Standing on the pegs helps.</li><li>Zero fuel between Valparai and Chalakudy (~120 km). Fill to the brim in Valparai.</li><li>Zero mobile signal in the forest; download offline maps for the whole loop.</li><li>Ride in a group of at least two bikes on the forest stretch — a breakdown there is a long wait.</li></ul></div></div>
<div><div class="rt-panel rt-timings"><h3>Timings, permits &amp; fuel</h3><ul><li>Forest check posts on the Valparai–Athirappilly road allow two-wheelers roughly 6:00 AM – 4:00 PM only (four-wheelers until 6:00 PM). Be at the Valparai side check post well before 3 PM.</li><li>No advance permit; small entry fee (₹20–30 for two-wheelers) and ID check at the check posts.</li><li>The forest road is closed intermittently after landslides in the monsoon (Jun–Sep) — check current status before leaving Pollachi.</li><li>Fuel: Pollachi, Valparai (last before the forest), then Chalakudy / Athirappilly town. Palakkad and Coimbatore are plentiful.</li></ul></div><div class="rt-panel rt-caution"><h3>Cautions</h3><ul><li>Elephants and gaur on the forest road — this stretch has regular human–wildlife incidents. Stop, engine off, reverse if needed. Never get between a calf and the herd.</li><li>Do not attempt the forest crossing in rain or fog; the drops are unguarded.</li><li>Leave Coimbatore by 5 AM to make the check-post cut-off with time for the falls.</li><li>The &#x27;most dangerous road&#x27; tag is about isolation and wildlife more than the surface — respect it.</li></ul></div></div>
