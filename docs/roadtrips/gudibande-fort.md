<p class="rt-summary">Gudibande Fort crowns a conical granite hill above a small town and lake in Chikkaballapur district. It was built in the 17th century by the local chieftain Byre Gowda, remembered as a Robin Hood who robbed the rich to help the poor. Around 400 broad stone steps climb through gateways and seven levels of walls past a chain of linked rock ponds to the Rameshwara temple on top. The ride is almost all on smooth NH 44, with a short run on district roads at the end.</p>

<div><div class="rt-panel "><h3>Highlights</h3><ul><li>17th-century hill fort of Byre Gowda, the local &#x27;Robin Hood&#x27;</li><li>19 rock ponds and tanks (&#x27;dhones&#x27;) linked so overflow fed the next, holding ~3 lakh litres</li><li>Seven levels of walls, gateways and sentry posts on the way up</li><li>Rameshwara (Shiva) temple at the summit, said to hold one of the 108 Jyotirlingas</li><li>Views over Gudibande Lake and the plains to Varlakonda</li></ul></div><div class="rt-panel rt-offroad"><h3>Off-road &amp; motorcycling notes</h3><div class="rt-rating" aria-label="Off-road rating 0 of 5"><i class=""></i><i class=""></i><i class=""></i><i class=""></i><i class=""></i></div><p class="rt-lead">Paved all the way to the base of the hill; the climb is on foot.</p><ul><li>Last stretch is narrow town lanes to the foothill — park near the base</li><li>Above the steps there is a short scramble over rock to the top</li></ul></div></div>
